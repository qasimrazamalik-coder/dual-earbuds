# Share this phone (phone to phone, party)

## How do I share my phone's music to other phones?
Open Dual Earbuds, tap **Share this phone**, then tap **Start sharing** and allow Android's sharing prompt. Other phones join with the QR code or the link, and hear what this phone plays.
1. Main screen > This phone as host > **Share this phone**.
2. Tap **Start sharing**.
3. When Android asks, allow it and choose to share the entire screen (Android uses this prompt for sound capture).
4. Start the music after tapping Start, or pause and play it again.
5. Other phones scan the QR code with Dual Earbuds or the camera; any browser can open the link.
**Android:** sharing needs Android 10 or newer on the host phone.

## Why does Android ask to share the screen when I share sound?
Android uses its screen-sharing prompt to allow copying other apps' sound. Dual Earbuds captures only sound, never the picture; if you cancel the prompt, sharing doesn't start.
1. Tap **Start sharing** again.
2. Choose to share the entire screen in Android's prompt.
**Android:** Android counts copying other apps' sound as recording, so it also asks for the audio permission. The microphone stays off unless you use Talk-over.

## Why can't I share Netflix from my phone?
Netflix and other protected video apps block sound capture, so listeners hear silence. This is an Android rule the app cannot change.
1. Share a laptop playing Netflix instead; that works.
2. On Samsung phones, use the built-in Dual Audio for protected apps (Quick panel > Media output > tick both earbuds).
3. YouTube, Spotify and most music apps share normally.

## How do I share only one app from my phone?
In Share this phone, under **Sound source**, set **Share** to **One app** and pick the app. Notifications then stay on this phone.
1. Share this phone > Sound source > **One app**.
2. Pick the app; use **Search apps** to find it, or **Change** to pick another.

## How does sync work when a phone is the host?
The host phone plays its own sound immediately and cannot delay it, so listeners line up with the host's ear. The host uses its own earbuds' calibrated delay; if they aren't calibrated it assumes 180 ms for Bluetooth and 20 ms for wired.
1. Calibrate the host phone's earbuds (Settings > **Calibrate earbuds**).
2. Ask every listener to calibrate theirs too.
**Android:** Share this phone > **Sync priority** offers Lip-sync and Together.

## How do I run a silent party from my phone?
Turn on **Party mode** in Share this phone: up to 16 listeners instead of 8, each with its own volume. Use this phone's hotspot or 5 GHz Wi-Fi.
1. Share this phone > Party > **Party mode**.
2. Optional: turn on **Room PIN** and type a 4 to 6 digit PIN; tell guests, it is never in the link or code.
3. Turn on this phone's hotspot and let guests join it (no router in between).
4. Guests scan the QR code; iPhone and other phones open the link in a browser.

## How do I talk to my listeners from the host phone?
Share this phone > Talk-over > **Talk to your listeners** mixes the phone's microphone into what they hear; the music drops 12 dB while it's on. Android asks for microphone permission the first time.
**Android:** **Mic noise cancelling** (Off, Light, Strong) removes fans, traffic and hum from your voice.

## How do I see or remove listeners on the host phone?
The **Ears** section lists this phone and every listener, with its earbuds, battery, sync and volume. Each listener has a volume slider and **Remove**.
1. Drag a listener's slider to set its volume (0 to 150 %).
2. Tap **Remove**, then confirm, to disconnect a listener.

## How do I share the link from the host phone?
Under **Join**, tap **Copy link** or **Share** to send it through any app. If the phone is on Wi-Fi and hotspot at once, pick the network your guests are on so the code uses the right address.
**Android:** **New link** disconnects everyone and the old code stops working.
