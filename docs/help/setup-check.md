# Setup check

## What is the Setup check?
Setup check lists everything on a device that makes sound and sync better or worse, each with a status, one plain sentence and a fix button. Start there when something doesn't work well.
**Windows:** the **Setup check** page in the left rail (also in the tray icon's menu); the rail shows how many items need a look. **Android:** Settings > **Setup check**, or the "Setup: N to check" chip on the main screen. **Android host:** the Setup check section in Share this phone.

## What does the Windows Setup check look at?
It checks Network type, Firewall rule, Wi-Fi band, Bluetooth earbuds, calibration of each output, Battery saver, Power mode and, while sharing, Listeners' Wi-Fi. Each row has a status and a button such as **Add rule**, **Network settings**, **Calibrate** or **Battery settings**.
1. Open **Setup check**.
2. Fix the rows that need a look, top to bottom.
3. Press **Check again**.
Below the checks, **For the best sound** lists five habits: same Wi-Fi or hotspot, 5 GHz, calibrate once, one room, AAC or SBC on Android.

## What does the Android listener Setup check look at?
It groups items under Background playback (Playing notification, Battery optimization, Background activity, Battery saver, your phone maker's steps), Earbuds (Bluetooth earbuds, Earbuds delay) and Network (Wi-Fi band, Same network as the host).
1. Settings > **Setup check**.
2. Tap each item's button to open the right Android screen.
3. For phone maker steps, tap **Mark done** when finished; Android can't tell apps whether those switches are set.

## What does the Share this phone Setup check look at?
On the host phone it checks battery optimisation, background use, battery saver, your phone maker's steps, notifications, the network (hotspot, Wi-Fi band, no network), weak listener links, and your own earbuds' calibration.
**Android host:** "Hotspot on" is best for parties; "Wi-Fi 2.4 GHz" is a warning; "Weak link" names listeners that make everyone wait.

## Why does Setup check say "Same network as the host: No"?
This phone and the host are on different networks, so they can't reach each other. Tap **Wi-Fi** on that row, join the host's Wi-Fi or hotspot, and come back; Setup check updates by itself.

## Why does Setup check say an output is not calibrated?
Without calibration the app uses an estimate, and Bluetooth delay differs per model by up to 100 ms, so ears may be slightly apart. Press **Calibrate** on that row and hold one earbud to the microphone.
**Windows:** a calibrated row offers **Measure again**. **Android:** "Earbuds delay: Not measured" opens Calibrate.

## Why does Setup check show the low-latency earbuds hint?
The earbuds measured more than 150 ms of delay. If they have a game or low-latency mode (often in the earbuds' own app or a triple tap), turn it on and calibrate again.
**Android:** also prefer AAC or SBC over LDAC (Developer options > Bluetooth audio codec).

## What does "Listeners' Wi-Fi" mean in Setup check?
It shows whether every listener's network keeps up with the shared deadline. If it names devices, those play later than the laptop (Lip-sync) or make everyone wait (Everyone together); move them closer or use 5 GHz or a hotspot.
