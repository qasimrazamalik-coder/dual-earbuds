# Getting started

## How do I play my laptop's sound on two pairs of earbuds?
Connect both pairs in Windows, then tick both of them under Outputs on Home and press Start sharing.
1. Open Windows Settings > Bluetooth & devices and pair each pair of earbuds.
2. In Dual Earbuds, open Home and tick each pair under Outputs.
3. Press Start sharing. The sound source is the Windows default output.
Platform notes: **Windows:** the pair that is the Windows default output is the sound source. **Android:** not needed.

## Why is the sound late on my earbuds?
Bluetooth earbuds add 150 to 250 ms of delay. Calibrate each pair so Dual Earbuds knows their real delay and lines them up.
1. Open Setup check.
2. Press Calibrate next to the earbuds and hold one earbud against the laptop's microphone.
3. Keep it still while 8 clicks play.

## How do I share the sound with a phone?
Open Listeners and scan the QR code with the phone's camera, or open the link in the phone's browser.
1. The phone must be on the same Wi-Fi as the laptop.
2. Scan the QR code on Listeners, or install the Dual Earbuds app and pick this laptop.

## The phone joined but there is no sound. What do I check?
Check that the phone and the laptop are on the same Wi-Fi and that Windows Firewall lets Dual Earbuds through on this network.
1. Open Setup check and look at the Firewall and Network rows.
2. On a public network, choose "Private and public networks" in Settings.
