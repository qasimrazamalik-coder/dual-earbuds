# Background play, screen off and battery

## Why does the sound stop when the phone screen turns off?
Some phones pause background apps to save battery. Let Dual Earbuds run unrestricted in the background and keep its playing notification on; the app's Setup check lists exactly what to change on your phone.
1. Settings > **Setup check** (or tap the "Setup: N to check" chip on the main screen).
2. Fix each item under **Background playback**: Playing notification, Battery optimization, Background activity, Battery saver.
3. Follow the phone maker steps (Settings > **Phone maker battery steps**).
4. Don't swipe the app away from Recents; lock it there if your phone offers that.

## How do I set Dual Earbuds to Unrestricted battery on Android?
Open App info for Dual Earbuds, go to Battery and choose Unrestricted, so Android doesn't pause the sound with the screen off.
1. Settings > Setup check > **Battery optimization** > open the fix, or long-press the app icon > App info.
2. **Battery** > **Unrestricted** (on some phones "Don't optimize").
3. Come back to Setup check; it checks again by itself when you return.

## How do I keep Dual Earbuds playing on a Samsung phone?
Set the app's battery to Unrestricted and add it to Never sleeping apps, so One UI doesn't put it to sleep.
1. App info > Battery > **Unrestricted**.
2. Device care > Battery > Background usage limits > **Never sleeping apps** > + > Dual Earbuds.
3. Make sure it is not in Sleeping or Deep sleeping apps.
4. On the same screen, switch off "Put unused apps to sleep" if you only use the app occasionally.
**Android:** the in-app **Open App info** and **Open Device care** buttons go straight there.

## How do I keep Dual Earbuds playing on Xiaomi, Redmi or POCO?
Xiaomi, Redmi and POCO phones close background apps aggressively. Allow Autostart and set Battery saver to No restrictions for Dual Earbuds.
1. Security > Permissions > **Autostart** > enable Dual Earbuds (button **Open Autostart**).
2. App info > Battery saver > **No restrictions** (button **Open battery saver**).
3. Open Recents, long-press Dual Earbuds (or swipe it down) and tap the lock icon.
4. Optional: App info > Other permissions > allow "Display pop-up windows while running in background"; some MIUI versions need it.

## How do I keep Dual Earbuds playing on Realme, OPPO or OnePlus?
Realme and OPPO phones pause background apps. Allow Auto launch and Allow background activity for Dual Earbuds.
1. Settings > Apps > **Auto launch** (or App management > Auto startup) > enable Dual Earbuds.
2. App info > Battery usage > turn on **Allow background activity** (and Allow foreground activity).
3. On older versions: Battery > Power saving > Dual Earbuds > allow.
4. Open Recents, tap the menu on Dual Earbuds and choose **Lock**.

## How do I keep Dual Earbuds playing on Vivo or iQOO?
Vivo and iQOO phones limit background apps. Allow background running and high background power use.
1. i Manager > App manager > Autostart / Background startup > enable Dual Earbuds.
2. Settings > Battery > Background power consumption management (or High background power consumption) > allow Dual Earbuds.
3. Open Recents and pull Dual Earbuds down to lock it.

## Does battery saver affect the sound?
Yes. Battery saver slows Wi-Fi and timers, which can cause stutters and more delay. Turn it off while sharing or listening, or plug in.
**Android:** the now playing panel warns "Battery saver is on. Turn it off if the sound stutters." **Windows:** Setup check > **Battery saver** and **Power mode**; Best power efficiency can delay audio threads, so Balanced or Best performance is smoother.

## Why does the host phone stop sharing in the background?
Android may pause sharing when the screen goes off unless the app may run without battery limits. Share this phone has its own Setup check with the fixes.
1. Share this phone > Setup check: fix "Battery optimisation is on" and "Background use is restricted" (App info > Battery > Unrestricted).
2. Allow notifications, so the sharing notification with a Stop button shows while the screen is off.
3. Follow your phone maker's steps above.

## Does the laptop keep sharing with the screen off or the window closed?
Yes. Closing the window keeps Dual Earbuds running in the tray, and while sharing or listening it asks Windows not to go to sleep and opts out of power throttling. Closing the lid still follows Windows' own lid setting.
**Windows:** Settings > General > **Closing the window** > **Keep running**.

## Why did the app ask "Keep playing with the screen off?"
On phones known to close background apps, the app offers this once so the sound keeps playing when the screen is off. Tap **Open Setup check** to see each step for your phone, or **Not now** to skip.
