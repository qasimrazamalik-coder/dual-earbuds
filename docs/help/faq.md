## Does Dual Earbuds work with Netflix?
Yes, when Netflix plays on the laptop, in the browser or the app. Netflix on an Android phone blocks other apps from capturing its sound, so phone-to-phone sharing can't include it.
**Android:** on Samsung phones, use the built-in Dual Audio for Netflix on the phone (Quick panel > Media output > tick both earbuds).

## Can I watch Netflix on a phone with two pairs of earbuds?
Not through Dual Earbuds, because Netflix blocks sound capture on Android. Watch on the laptop and share from there, or on a Samsung phone use Samsung Dual Audio, which connects two Bluetooth pairs to the phone directly.

## What is Samsung Dual Audio and should I use it instead?
Dual Audio is a Samsung feature that plays the phone's sound on two Bluetooth devices at once, including protected apps like Netflix. Use it for two pairs on one Samsung phone; use Dual Earbuds for more pairs, for other phones and laptops, or when the sound comes from a laptop.
**Android:** Quick panel > Media output > tick both earbuds.

## Can Windows play to two Bluetooth earbuds at once by itself?
Usually not. Most laptop Bluetooth chips send stereo music to only one device at a time, and Windows refuses the second pair (error 0x8889000F). Dual Earbuds uses a phone or another laptop for every extra pair, or a second Bluetooth radio.

## Do I need a USB Bluetooth adapter for two pairs on one laptop?
Only if you want both pairs on the laptop itself. A second USB Bluetooth adapter gives Windows a second radio, so each pair can have its own output on Home; otherwise let the second pair listen through a phone, which needs no extra hardware.
**Windows:** pair the second earbuds through the adapter; they then appear under **Outputs** on Home.

## Is there an iPhone app?
Not yet. iPhones can listen today by opening the host's link in the browser, but an iPhone cannot share its own sound.

## Does Dual Earbuds cost anything?
No. Dual Earbuds is free and open source (MIT license), with no ads, no account, no subscription and no cloud costs. It uses no mobile data for the sound.

## Can I use wired headphones or speakers instead of earbuds?
Yes. Any output works: Bluetooth earbuds, wired headphones, the laptop's speakers or the phone's speaker. Wired outputs add almost no delay; Bluetooth adds more, so calibrate Bluetooth pairs.

## Can each person have their own volume?
Yes. Every listener sets its own volume on its device, and on the laptop each output has its own Volume slider. In Party mode the host can also set each listener's volume from its listener list.

## Can I use it for a silent disco?
Yes. Turn on Party mode on the host for up to 16 listeners, use a hotspot or 5 GHz Wi-Fi, and choose **Everyone together** so every ear plays at the same moment. Guests join from the app or straight from a browser.

## Who makes Dual Earbuds?
Dual Earbuds is made by Qasim Raza Malik and published as open source on GitHub (qasimrazamalik-coder/dual-earbuds). The About page shows the version and how it works.
